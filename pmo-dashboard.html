Dashboard / PMO

PMO Delivery Health Dashboard.

An executive-ready Tableau portfolio view designed to connect project health, sprint delivery, milestones, RAID items, and dependencies to clear stakeholder reporting.

From source definitions to executive decisions

The project combines a KPI framework, supporting documentation, and interactive Tableau views. Together, the artifacts show how delivery data can be structured for recurring status reviews, escalation, and portfolio-level decisions.

Structuring project, sprint, work-item, RAID, and dependency data for analysis.

03

Designing executive and delivery views with drilldowns for programs, teams, and projects.
